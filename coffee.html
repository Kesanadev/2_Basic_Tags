About Coffee

Coffee is a brewed drink prepared from roasted coffee beans, the seeds of berries from certain Coffea species. When coffee berries turn from green to yellow, then to orange, and finally to a deep, glossy red, they are ready to be picked, processed, and dried.

Popular Coffee Types

Espresso

Strong coffee brewed by forcing hot water through finely-ground coffee beans.

Cappuccino

Espresso with steamed milk and foam, typically in equal parts.

Latte

Espresso with more steamed milk and a light layer of foam.

Americano

Espresso diluted with hot water, similar to drip coffee.
